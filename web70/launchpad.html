How to connect:

Connect your host computer to Wi-Fi, Ethernet or a hotspot. Puppy50 uses its virtual Ethernet adapter to share the host's connection through QEMU NAT. In this preview, the VM does not directly select Wi-Fi networks.

For the address bar, press Ctrl+L, enter an HTTPS address and press Enter. Nothing on this page automatically opens a website.

This is experimental software. Avoid sensitive accounts until browser update and security audits are complete. Your VM session is not saved to disk.
