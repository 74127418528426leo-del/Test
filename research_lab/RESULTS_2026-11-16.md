## Latest matched prefetch comparison

[Run 43874157306](https://github.com/74127418528426leo-del/Test/actions/runs/43874157306),
artifact 14229756794, compared four cold boots per condition on the same
runner (two BIOS and two UEFI; 3072 MiB guest RAM). The 256 MiB control
reported 7.71, 11.07, 7.93, and 10.86 s (mean 9.393 s). The 32 MiB
candidate reported 9.55, 10.85, 7.71, and 11.04 s (mean 9.787 s), or
0.395 s slower on the reported mean. Paired differences were mixed. All
eight boots passed the recorded browser, RAM migration, media-eject, and
post-eject local HTTP 200 gates; none met the strict under-five-second
goal. These are sampled home-page detections, not exact Firefox paint.
The compare job also verified both original kernel paths, the root, and
fallback against the authenticated base ISO. The separate state-record
job failed with HTTP 403; the run was later added to persistent state
manually. The goal flag remains false.

## Critical path and measurement caveats

In all ten boots the guest timestamps put the interval from entry into the
early X11 launcher until its existing `mcookie` call returned at 1.00–1.02 s.
The reason for the duration is not established. In these same samples the
host first observed the early-user marker at a median 2.883 s for BIOS and
7.517 s for UEFI. This locates a large firmware-dependent difference before
the early-user marker; it does not separate firmware from bootloader or
kernel startup. The interval between host receipt of the Firefox execution
marker and host observation of the rendered home page was approximately
3.88–4.52 s, including observation delays. These are host-to-host
measurements; guest uptime is never subtracted from host monotonic time.

The earlier matched run `43777265230` reported four UEFI home detections of
13.55 s. Its serial logs and first-home screenshots differ across boots, and
the benchmark has no 13.55-second timeout. Its 0.2-second screenshot
polling and two-decimal rounding can produce repeated values. The older
artifact lacks per-probe timestamps, so the precise reason for the equality
cannot be recovered retrospectively.
